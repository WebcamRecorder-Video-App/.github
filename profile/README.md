## 🚀 At a Glance
Webcam Recorder for Windows is a focused desktop app for recording video from your camera. Use it on its own to capture a talking-head clip, a reaction, or a personal video message, with microphone audio recorded in step with the picture. When a project calls for more, the same webcam and screen recorder captures your desktop while placing your camera as an overlay — the standard look for tutorials and demonstrations. Recording is local and every clip saves to a video file on your PC, so exporting and sharing is on your terms.

## 🎯 Quick Start
1. Open Webcam Recorder and select camera-only or screen-with-webcam recording.
2. Choose the camera and microphone you want and check the live preview.
3. For combined captures, position the webcam overlay so it stays out of the way.
4. Press record, using pause whenever you need a moment.
5. Stop to finish and save the clip to your computer for review or sharing.
